Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We want you to be confident working with us. This policy explains when refunds apply to our services.

Template notice: this is a starting template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer for your jurisdiction before publishing.

1. Milestone-Based Delivery

Projects are delivered in milestones that you review and approve. This lets you raise concerns early, before further work and payment.

2. Deposits

Deposits reserve team capacity and cover initial discovery and planning. If you cancel before any work has started, we refund the deposit minus any costs already incurred.

3. Approved Milestones

Payments for milestones you have approved are non-refundable, because the work has been delivered and accepted.

4. Unmet Specifications

If a deliverable does not meet the agreed specification, we will fix it at no extra cost. If we cannot deliver what was agreed, you will not pay for that milestone.

5. Founding Client Offer

Under the founding-client offer, you pay nothing until you approve the working demo. If you do not approve the demo, you owe nothing for that build.

6. Subscriptions and Retainers

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. Fees already paid for the current month are not refunded.

7. Third-Party Costs

Costs paid to third parties on your behalf (such as domains, licences or app store fees) are non-refundable.

8. How to Request a Refund

Email our email with your project name and the reason for your request. We reply within 5 business days.
